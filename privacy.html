Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The Open Path respects your privacy. This page describes our intended handling of information when ordering is enabled. The current website is a preview: the request form does not yet transmit orders or process payments.

Information you provide

When ordering becomes available, we may collect your name, email address, optional birth date, selected intention, and the request you choose to share. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not necessary for your ceremony.

How information is used

We intend to use your information to process your request, communicate with you, and provide the ceremony and agreed confirmation. Payment details will be processed by an external payment provider rather than stored on this website.

Sharing and retention

We will publish the names of our actual form, order, and payment service providers, and our retention and deletion practices, before accepting orders.

Terms of Service

The Open Path offers personalized symbolic spiritual ceremonies. No specific life event, financial result, relationship outcome, or other external result is promised. The precise deliverables, timeframe, and payment terms will be stated before checkout is activated.

Refund Policy

Refund eligibility, cancellation deadlines, and how to request a refund will be published before any paid orders are accepted. Applicable consumer rights remain in effect.
